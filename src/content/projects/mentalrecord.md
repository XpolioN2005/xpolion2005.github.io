## Overview

Mental Record is a short narrative-driven mystery game built with Godot. Players interrogate characters, analyze their statements, and identify contradictions to uncover the truth.

I worked as a core programmer on the four-person development team, focusing on the game's interaction and gameplay systems. I implemented the player interaction and statement systems, built the dialogue and conversation framework, and developed the save and load system.

I also implemented the game's 3D environments on the technical side, working with the existing assets.

The game was developed and released for Windows, Linux, and Web.
